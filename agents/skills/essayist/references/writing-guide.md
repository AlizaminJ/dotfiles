# Blog writing guidance

These are editorial choices, not a reusable outline. Let the material and intended
reader determine the article. Don't make every post sound like the last one, but
don't force novelty when a familiar approach genuinely fits.

## Find the angle

Decide what makes this piece worth reading: a useful fix, a surprising observation,
a practical technique, a tradeoff, or a clearer explanation. Stay focused on that
point instead of recounting everything that happened in the session.

## Let structure follow the topic

- A debugging story might follow the discovery process, or reveal the fix first
  and explain how you reached it. Include the steps needed to understand the cause,
  not every dead end. Say how the result was checked.
- A practical how-to might lead with the result, then show the setup and caveats.
- A design decision might compare alternatives, follow a worked example, or explore
  a tension between competing goals. Explain why the chosen approach fits.
- A concept piece might build an analogy or follow a question through several examples.

Mix approaches where useful. A small troubleshooting detour doesn't need its own
investigation section: weave it into the explanation, use a brief aside, or omit it.
Use headings when they help navigation, not to fill a standard set of sections.
A short article can work as continuous prose.

## Vary voice, openings, and endings

Choose a voice appropriate to the reader and subject: personal reflection, direct
instruction, analytical comparison, or something else the user requests. First
person is useful for genuine experiences; don't invent personal anecdotes.

An opening can start with a result, a concrete moment, a question, or the problem.
Get to something interesting promptly without requiring the same kind of hook.
Avoid filler introductions and generic claims about how important the topic is.

End where the point lands: a useful next step, an implication, a limitation, an
unresolved tradeoff, or a reader question if it adds something. Don't automatically
append a takeaway heading, summary, or invitation to comment to every article.

## Keep the substance strong

- Use accurate, concrete details. Don't invent facts, outcomes, or certainty to fill gaps.
- Show short code/config/log snippets when they clarify the explanation. Explain how
  to use the example; link to longer implementations rather than pasting large files.
- Use plain language and explain necessary technical terms without flattening the
  user's own voice or making every article equally casual.
- Keep the pace readable. Cut repetition and filler, not the context needed to understand
  the reasoning. Let the requested depth determine length rather than padding sections.

Before delivering, check whether the angle is clear, the examples support it, and
any repeated opening, heading pattern, or ending is a deliberate fit rather than habit.
